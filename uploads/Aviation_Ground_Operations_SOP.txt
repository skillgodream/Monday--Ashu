SOP — Aviation Ground Operations
Process: Passenger Boarding & Aircraft Turnaround Support | Role: Ground Operations Agent

Purpose: Support safe, orderly and on-time aircraft turnaround and passenger boarding while following airport, airline and security procedures.

1. ORIENT — Understand the Operation
Objective: Complete assigned ground-operation activities safely, accurately and within the required turnaround timeline.
• Work only within the responsibilities and authority assigned to the role.
• Follow current airline, airport, security and safety procedures.
• Safety and security take priority over schedule pressure.

2. PREPARE — Before Boarding / Turnaround
• Check assigned flight, gate, scheduled departure time and current operational status.
• Confirm the required communication channel and assigned supervisor/turnaround coordinator.
• Check that passenger, gate and boarding information is available.
• Confirm the gate/boarding area is ready and access routes are clear.
• Ensure required identification, communication equipment and authorised equipment are available and serviceable.

3. PERFORM — Passenger Boarding
• Confirm readiness: Receive authorised instruction to begin boarding.
• Prepare passengers: Make clear boarding announcements and provide required instructions.
• Verify: Check boarding documents using the approved process.
• Control flow: Manage the queue and maintain orderly passenger movement.
• Monitor: Identify passengers requiring assistance and coordinate authorised support.
• Close: Follow the approved process for final passenger reconciliation and boarding closure.

4. CONTROL — Safety, Security & Accuracy
• Maintain clear passenger movement routes and emergency access.
• Do not allow unauthorised access to restricted or controlled areas.
• Follow approved identity, boarding-document and passenger verification procedures.
• Report unattended or suspicious items immediately through the authorised security process.
• Do not bypass security controls because of time pressure.
• Use only authorised equipment and follow airside safety rules.
CRITICAL RULE: A departure deadline never overrides an aviation safety or security control.

5. DECIDE — Operational Judgement
• Passenger document issue: Do not improvise; follow the approved verification/escalation process.
• Special assistance: Coordinate authorised support before proceeding.
• Gate or flight information changes: Confirm the current instruction through the authorised operational channel.
• Security concern: Do not investigate beyond your role; follow the approved security escalation process.

6. HANDLE — Exceptions
• Document mismatch: pause the affected passenger's boarding and seek authorised resolution.
• Wrong gate/flight: verify current flight information before directing the passenger.
• Boarding system unavailable: use only the approved contingency process.
• Boarding delay: communicate through the designated operational channel and follow revised instructions.
• Unattended item or suspicious behaviour: maintain appropriate distance and initiate the authorised security response.

7. ESCALATE — Stop & Seek Authorised Support
• Security concern or unauthorised access.
• Unattended or suspicious item.
• Passenger identity or boarding-document issue that cannot be resolved within your authority.
• Safety hazard in the gate/boarding area.
• Medical emergency or passenger requiring urgent assistance.
• Equipment/system failure affecting safe boarding.
• Conflicting operational instructions.
• Any situation where it is unclear whether it is safe or authorised to continue.
Escalation Rule: STOP/HOLD → PROTECT PEOPLE & SECURITY → INFORM AUTHORISED PERSON → RESUME ONLY WHEN CLEARED.

8. FINAL TURNAROUND / BOARDING CHECK
• Passenger boarding status is reconciled according to the approved process.
• Any unresolved passenger, safety or security issue has been escalated.
• Gate and boarding area remain controlled and safe.
• Required operational communication is complete.
• Flight is released/closed only through the authorised process.

9. FINAL QUALITY GATE
• Was every boarding verification completed correctly?
• Were safety and security controls maintained?
• Were exceptions handled through the approved process?
• Were unresolved issues escalated correctly?
• Was final passenger/boarding status accurately reconciled?
Definition of Good Performance: Safe + secure + accurate + orderly + compliant + timely.

NEVER
• Bypass identity, boarding or security controls to save time.
• Allow unauthorised access to restricted areas.
• Handle or investigate a suspicious item beyond your authorised procedure.
• Ignore a safety hazard because boarding is delayed.
• Give passengers instructions based on unverified information.
• Continue when an unresolved safety or security concern requires escalation.
